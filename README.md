This repository contains the source code for my personal academic website. The website showcases my research, publications, and academic activities as a Ph.D. student at Nanjing University.

## About Me

I am currently a first-year Ph.D. student in the School of Intelligence Science and Technology at Nanjing University, supervised by Professor Wenbin Li. Before transferring to the Ph.D. program through the integrated Master's–Ph.D. track, I studied as a master's student in the School of Computer Science at Nanjing University. I am a member of the Reasoning and Learning Research Group, led by Professor Yang Gao.

## Research Interests

- Machine Learning
- Continual Learning
- Model Merging

## Website Structure

The website includes the following sections:

- **About Me**: Brief introduction about my academic background
- **News**: Recent updates about my research and academic activities
- **Preprints**: My preprint papers
- **Publications**: My published papers in academic journals and conferences
- **Awards**: Academic awards and honors I've received
- **Services**: My contributions as a reviewer and organizer for academic conferences
